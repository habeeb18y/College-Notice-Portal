🎓 College Notice Portal

A web-based notice management system that enables administrators, faculty members, and students to communicate through a centralized digital platform.

 📌 Overview

The College Notice Portal is designed to replace traditional notice boards with a digital platform. It allows administrators and faculty members to publish notices while students can access announcements relevant to their classes in real time.

The system provides role-based access control with separate dashboards for Admin, Faculty, and Student users.
